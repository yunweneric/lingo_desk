# Provider marks

Trademarks of their respective owners, included solely to identify each
provider in the AI providers screen. LingoDesk is not affiliated with or
endorsed by any of them.

| File | Provider | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `anthropic.svg` | Anthropic (Claude) | `https://claude.ai/favicon.svg` |
| `openai.svg` | OpenAI | Wikimedia Commons, "OpenAI logo 2025 (symbol).svg" |
| `gemini.svg` | Google Gemini | `https://www.gstatic.com/lamda/images/gemini_sparkle_v002_d4735304ff6292a690345.svg` |

`openai.svg` is the upstream artwork with `fill="currentColor"` added to its
single path, so the mark can be drawn black on light and white on dark as
OpenAI's brand guidelines require. The other two keep their brand colours,
which carry across both themes unchanged.
